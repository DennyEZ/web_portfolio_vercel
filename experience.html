Software Sub-Team Member — ITU AUV Team

2024 – present  |  Istanbul Technical University  |  github.com/itu-auv

Member of the software sub-team of a student team that designs, builds and operates an autonomous underwater vehicle for international robotics competitions. The vehicle must complete its tasks entirely without operator input.

Responsibilities and contributions

  • Developed a synthetic dataset generator producing photorealistic, automatically annotated training data for pose estimation and segmentation.
  • Worked on computer vision pipelines for detecting competition targets in degraded underwater imagery.
  • Integrated perception output with the ROS-based navigation stack and validated behaviour in simulation before pool testing.
  • Coordinated with the mechanical and electrical sub-teams on sensor mounting, thruster interfaces and system-level integration.
Technologies: ROS, Python, C++, OpenCV, Linux, simulation, computer vision
