Welcome

My name is Deniz Meral and I am an undergraduate student of Control and Automation Engineering at Istanbul Technical University. This site is a plain, straightforward record of my technical work: the control systems I study, the electronics I build, and the software that ties the two together.

My interest sits at the point where theory meets hardware. A transfer function is only useful once it is discretised, written into firmware, and made to drive a real actuator against real noise and real sensor lag. Most of what is documented on this site was built to close that gap — motor control, sensor conditioning, embedded firmware, industrial automation logic, and the simulation work that comes before any of it is trusted on hardware.

Note: I am currently looking for a summer internship in control systems, industrial automation, or embedded electronics. My CV is available on request — please use the contact page.

Areas of Work

Area Description
Control Systems Classical and modern control: PID tuning, root locus, frequency response, state-space modelling, observers, and discrete-time implementation on microcontrollers.
Embedded Electronics STM32 and AVR firmware in C, timer and interrupt driven peripherals, ADC sampling, PWM generation, and serial protocols (UART, I²C, SPI, CAN).
Industrial Automation Ladder logic and structured text for PLCs, sensor and actuator interfacing, HMI/SCADA concepts, and safety-oriented sequence design.
Instrumentation Signal conditioning, amplification and filtering for strain, temperature, current and pressure sensors; noise reduction and calibration procedures.
Robotics Software ROS-based navigation and perception for an autonomous underwater vehicle, including simulation and synthetic data generation.
